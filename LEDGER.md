# Ideato Neki ledger

Ideato's project ledger: Neki earned for work on Ideato (ideato.social). A person's share of Ideato = their Neki here / all Neki here. Rules: TOPC's NEKI.md. Tasks: the issues in this repo; an accepted task becomes a row here.

Units: 1 Neki = 1 rupee of work at today's market price (what it costs to get the result from a competent mid-level person in India working with AI), priced from TOPC's rate card (`ecosystem/RATE_CARD.md` in the topc repo). Rows are only ever added, never edited; corrections are new rows. Status is `provisional` until an outside judge reviews it, then `final`.
